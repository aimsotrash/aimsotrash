### Experience

<picture><source media="(max-width: 481px)" srcset="assets/experience-narrow.svg"><source media="(min-width: 482px) and (max-width: 602px), (min-width: 768px) and (max-width: 890px)" srcset="assets/experience-small.svg"><source media="(min-width: 603px) and (max-width: 767px), (min-width: 891px) and (max-width: 1133px)" srcset="assets/experience-medium.svg"><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/experience-dark.svg"><img alt="Experience: DevOps Engineer at HP Inc, Pune, August 2022 to December 2025; DevOps Engineer Intern at HP Inc, March to July 2022." src="assets/experience-light.svg" width="100%"></picture>

At HP I administered the AWS account behind its commercial device-telemetry platform.

- The account ingested telemetry from millions of enterprise devices through Kinesis into a petabyte-scale, five-tier lake on S3, Hive/RDS and Redshift. I provisioned EMR jobs and infrastructure for new sources, as often as daily during device launches.
- Automated GDPR erasure with Airflow-orchestrated Python, replacing manual ingestion pauses and hand-restarted deletion jobs. Turnaround went from up to a month to 48 hours, inside the two-week compliance window.
- Managed the account's infrastructure as code with Terraform and Terragrunt, including moving Terraform state out of a relinquished AWS account.
- Built Azure DevOps pipelines that deploy Terraform, Lambda and EMR jobs to AWS from a git commit.
- Wrote IAM roles and policies from scratch, including cross-account data-sharing roles, and owned the remediation of security findings.
- Shared the production on-call rotation, triaging job and pipeline failures through CloudWatch alarms and PagerDuty.
